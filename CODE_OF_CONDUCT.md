# Code of Conduct

Traetoy welcomes constructive participation. Be respectful, assume good faith,
and focus discussion on the project and its users.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, publication of private data, and
hostile personal attacks are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, device
identities, private logs, or unpatched vulnerability details.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject content that violates these rules.
Report conduct concerns through the private contact requested in SECURITY.md.
